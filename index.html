how it works

Traders pay. Landlords collect.

01

List

Name a property, pick the stock it pays in, set the rent wallet. Launch it on Pons with the RENT description.

02

Rent

Every trade on the property pays 1% rent. 0.7% goes to the rent wallet, 0.3% to Pons. Curve fees before graduation, pool fees after.

03

Collect

Every hour the rent wallet swaps what it holds to $USDG, buys the stock, and sends it to every holder's wallet, pro rata.

04

Cap rate

Each property is priced like real estate: 24h rent ÷ market cap, annualized. Read from the chain, never typed in.
